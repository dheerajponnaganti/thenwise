# Code of conduct

Thenwise follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repository, Discussions, issues, pull requests and anywhere else you speak for the project.

## In short

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Welcome people of every background and every level of experience.
- Give and take feedback on the work, never on the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, or sharing someone's private details.

## Reporting

Email **dheerajponnaganti@gmail.com** with what happened, where, and links if you have them. Every report is read and kept private.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block anyone who breaks this code, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
