Terms we keep mixing up.

- Cursor: the last change id a device has seen ([[Track sync progress with a per-device cursor]]).
- Operation: one queued edit ([[Offline Mode]]).
- Canonical row: the server's version of a record ([[Conflict Resolution]]).
- Train: the two week release cycle.
